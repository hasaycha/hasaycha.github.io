Hasaycha Assistant: Privacy Policy / Kebijakan Privasi

Last updated / Terakhir diperbarui: 14 November 2026 · Contact / Kontak: hasaychadan@gmail.com · About the app

English

Hasaycha Assistant is a private tool made and run by CodenameHRT, an independent developer in Indonesia, for his own Google accounts only. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • Data it accesses: with the owner's consent through Google sign-in, his own Gmail (to read unread messages, add labels and create drafts), YouTube channel statistics and uploads, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and contacts, only when he asks for a task that needs them.
  • How it is used: to sort his mail, prepare replies he reviews and sends himself, report on his own channels and publish the videos he approved. The app never sends email by itself.
  • Where it is kept: on the owner's own computers. Short message excerpts may be processed by an AI model to sort them and draft replies; nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or given to third parties.
  • Google user data: the use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; stored data is deleted on request to the contact above.

Bahasa Indonesia

Hasaycha Assistant adalah alat pribadi buatan dan kelolaan CodenameHRT, pengembang independen di Indonesia, hanya untuk akun Google miliknya sendiri. Tidak ditawarkan kepada orang lain.

  • Data yang diakses: dengan izin pemilik lewat login Google, Gmail miliknya (membaca email belum dibaca, memberi label, membuat draf), statistik dan unggahan kanal YouTube-nya, Kalender, Drive, Docs, Sheets dan kontak, hanya saat ia meminta tugas yang membutuhkannya.
  • Penggunaan: merapikan email, menyiapkan balasan yang ia periksa dan kirim sendiri, melaporkan kanal miliknya, dan mengunggah video yang sudah ia setujui. Aplikasi tidak pernah mengirim email sendiri.
  • Penyimpanan: di komputer milik pemilik sendiri. Cuplikan pesan dapat diproses model AI untuk menyortir dan menyusun draf; tidak ada yang dijual, dibagikan untuk iklan, atau diberikan ke pihak ketiga.
  • Penghapusan: akses bisa dicabut kapan saja di myaccount.google.com/permissions; data yang tersimpan dihapus atas permintaan ke kontak di atas.
