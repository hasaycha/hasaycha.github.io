Hasaycha Assistant

A private assistant made and run by CodenameHRT for his own accounts. It is not offered to the public. Contact: hasaychadan@gmail.com · Privacy policy / Kebijakan privasi

What it does

  • Sorts the owner's own unread Gmail into labels and prepares reply drafts that he reviews and sends himself. It never sends email on its own.
  • Reads statistics of the owner's own YouTube channels and publishes the videos he approved.
  • Reads and writes the owner's own calendar, documents and spreadsheets when he asks.

Apa ini

Asisten pribadi buatan CodenameHRT untuk akun-akunnya sendiri: merapikan Gmail dengan label dan menyiapkan draf balasan (tidak pernah mengirim sendiri), membaca statistik kanal YouTube sendiri dan mengunggah video yang sudah ia setujui, serta membantu kalender dan dokumen miliknya. Tidak ditawarkan untuk umum.
