Bahasa Indonesia

Cara meminta penghapusan data

  1. Kirim email ke hasaychadan@gmail.com dengan subjek Hapus data Gemoy Grid Puzzle.
  2. Tulis nama tampilan yang Anda pakai di papan skor (dan perkiraan tanggal Anda bermain). Jika Anda punya ID pemain, sertakan juga. Kami memakainya untuk menemukan data Anda. Kami tidak meminta kata sandi, nomor telepon, atau data pribadi lain.
  3. Kami membalas untuk mengonfirmasi, lalu menghapus data. Paling lambat 60 hari setelah permintaan diterima.

Data yang dihapus

  • Nama pemain (beserta jumlah perubahannya; nama itu menjadi bebas dipakai pemain lain), ID pemain acak, dan semua skor Anda di papan skor.
  • Catatan permainan (bibit acak, urutan langkah dan waktu) dan alamat IP yang tersimpan bersama skor.
  • Cadangan progres: bintang, level, rentetan harian, hari bermain, misi, skin, dan hash kode pemulihan.

Data yang disimpan

  • Cadangan harian server memuat salinan data di atas. Salinan itu terhapus otomatis dalam 7 hari setelah data utama dihapus.
  • Data di perangkat Anda (pengaturan, skor tertinggi) tidak ada di server kami. Hapus dengan mencopot pemasangan game atau menghapus data aplikasi.
  • Data iklan dikelola oleh Google AdMob. Anda dapat mengatur atau mereset ID iklan di pengaturan Android.

Kami tidak menyimpan data lain setelah penghapusan selesai.

English

How to request deletion of your data

  1. Email hasaychadan@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Gemoy Grid Puzzle data.
  2. Include the display name you use on the leaderboard (and roughly when you played). If you have your player ID, include it too. We use it only to find your data. We do not ask for a password, phone number or other personal data.
  3. We reply to confirm and then delete the data, within 60 days of receiving your request.

Data that is deleted

  • Your player name (with its change count; the name becomes free for other players), random player ID and all your leaderboard scores.
  • Gameplay logs (random seed, moves and timings) and the IP address stored with scores.
  • Progress backup: stars, level, daily streak, days played, missions, skins and the hash of your recovery code.

Data that is kept

  • Daily server backups hold a copy of the data above. Those copies are deleted automatically within 7 days after the main data is removed.
  • Data on your device (settings, high scores) is not on our servers. Remove it by uninstalling the game or clearing app data.
  • Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob. You can control or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

We keep no other data once deletion is complete.
