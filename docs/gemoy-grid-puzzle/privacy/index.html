Bahasa Indonesia

1. Siapa kami

Gemoy Grid Puzzle dibuat dan dikelola oleh CodenameHRT, pengembang independen di Indonesia. Dalam kebijakan ini, "kami" berarti CodenameHRT. Kami bertanggung jawab atas data pribadi yang dijelaskan di sini. Kontak: hasaychadan@gmail.com.

2. Ringkasan

Game ini tidak membutuhkan akun, email, atau nomor telepon. Game memakai internet untuk lima hal: papan skor online, cadangan progres untuk pindah HP, iklan Google AdMob, pemeriksaan keaslian aplikasi (Google Play Integrity), dan statistik penggunaan anonim. Pengaturan dan skor tertinggi Anda juga disimpan di perangkat.

3. Data yang kami proses dan untuk apa

a. Papan skor online. Saat Anda menyelesaikan game, game mengirim ke server kami:

  • ID pemain acak (UUID) yang dibuat game di perangkat Anda. Ini bukan nama asli, email, atau ID iklan.
  • Nama pemain yang Anda pilih (3 sampai 12 karakter). Nama itu unik (tidak ada dua pemain dengan nama yang sama) dan dipakai tetap untuk seterusnya. Mengubahnya hanya bisa lewat Pengaturan dengan menonton iklan berhadiah, dan jumlah iklan yang harus ditonton bertambah satu setiap kali nama diubah. Server menyimpan nama, jumlah perubahan, dan waktu perubahan terakhir. Nama dan skor tampil di papan skor yang dapat dilihat semua pemain, jadi pilih nama yang Anda nyaman tampilkan.
  • Skor, jumlah langkah, lama bermain, mode permainan, dan waktu permainan.
  • Catatan permainan: bibit acak game, urutan langkah dan waktunya. Dipakai untuk memeriksa bahwa skor didapat secara sah (anti-curang).
  • Alamat IP saat permintaan, untuk keamanan dan pembatasan penyalahgunaan.

Skor yang belum terkirim (misalnya saat offline) disimpan sementara di perangkat dan dikirim saat koneksi kembali.

b. Cadangan progres (Pengaturan > Cadangan / pindah HP). Server menyimpan ID pemain, bintang, level, streak harian, jumlah hari bermain, dan misi yang selesai, serta hash dari kode pemulihan acak Anda (bukan kodenya). Tujuannya agar progres bisa dipulihkan di perangkat lain. Kode pemulihan adalah rahasia Anda: siapa pun yang memilikinya dapat memulihkan progres Anda, jadi jangan membagikannya.

c. Iklan. Game menampilkan iklan lewat Google AdMob. Lihat bagian 5.

d. Data teknis. Log server (permintaan dan kesalahan) dapat memuat ID pemain dan alamat IP. Kami memakainya untuk keamanan dan memperbaiki masalah.

e. Google Play. Jika Anda memasang dari Google Play, Google memberi kami statistik agregat dan laporan crash/ANR dari pengguna yang mengaktifkan berbagi data diagnostik. Itu dikelola Google.

f. Pemeriksaan keaslian aplikasi (Google Play Integrity). Saat mengirim skor atau memilih dan mengubah nama, game dapat meminta sebuah token kepada Google Play di perangkat Anda. Token itu dikirim ke server kami, lalu kami meneruskannya ke Google untuk diperiksa: apakah aplikasinya yang resmi dari Google Play dan perangkatnya asli. Google memproses sinyal tentang perangkat dan aplikasi Anda untuk pemeriksaan ini sesuai kebijakan Google. Kami hanya mencatat hasilnya (lolos atau tidak) di log server, bukan tokennya. Tujuannya keamanan dan pencegahan kecurangan. Bila pemeriksaan tidak tersedia (misalnya tanpa Google Play), game tetap dapat dimainkan.

g. Statistik penggunaan anonim. Game menjumlahkan di perangkat Anda hal-hal seperti berapa kali game dibuka, lama bermain, jumlah game per mode, jumlah iklan yang tampil, dan hadiah yang diambil, lalu mengirim jumlahnya saja ke server kami bersama nomor hari, hari pertama game dipasang, versi game, dan nama kelompok uji pengaturan. Kiriman ini tidak memuat ID pemain, nama, ID iklan, atau ID perangkat, dan server hanya menyimpan angka gabungan per hari sehingga tidak bisa ditelusuri ke Anda. Alamat IP terlihat oleh server saat permintaan (untuk membatasi penyalahgunaan) tetapi tidak disimpan bersama statistik. Kami memakainya untuk mengetahui apakah pemain kembali bermain dan untuk menyetel game.

h. Pengaturan dari server. Saat dibuka, game mengambil angka pengaturan (misalnya jarak antar iklan) dari server kami. Permintaan ini tidak membawa data tentang Anda.

4. Data yang hanya ada di perangkat Anda

Skor tertinggi lokal, pengaturan (suara, getar, tampilan, bahasa), misi harian, hadiah harian yang sudah diambil, dan profil permainan: skor terbaru Anda, kapan terakhir bermain, dan apakah Anda sering menonton iklan berhadiah. Profil ini dipakai di perangkat untuk mengatur tingkat kesulitan dan seberapa sering iklan ditawarkan. Data ini tidak dikirim ke server kami (kecuali yang disebut di bagian 3).

5. Iklan dan persetujuan (Google AdMob)

AdMob dapat mengumpulkan dan memakai ID iklan, alamat IP, data perangkat dan penggunaan, serta interaksi dengan iklan sesuai kebijakan Google. Di wilayah yang mewajibkan (misalnya Wilayah Ekonomi Eropa dan Inggris) game menampilkan dialog persetujuan (UMP) sebelum iklan dimuat. Anda dapat mengubah pilihan itu kapan saja di Pengaturan > Opsi privasi. Anda juga dapat mereset atau menghapus ID iklan di pengaturan Android (Google > Iklan).

Iklan berhadiah bersifat pilihan. Kami tidak meminta, mendorong, atau memberi hadiah untuk klik iklan.

Izin Android yang diminta game: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, VIBRATE, dan AD_ID (ID iklan).

6. Dasar pemrosesan

Kami memproses data berdasarkan: (1) persetujuan Anda, untuk iklan di wilayah yang mewajibkan; (2) kepentingan yang sah, untuk menjalankan papan skor, keamanan, dan anti-curang; (3) pelaksanaan layanan yang Anda pilih, untuk cadangan progres.

  • Google AdMob, untuk iklan (bagian 5). Kebijakan Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy dan https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

  • Pihak berwenang, bila diwajibkan hukum.

Kami tidak memakai layanan analitik atau pelaporan crash pihak ketiga di dalam game.

Halaman kebijakan ini disajikan oleh GitHub Pages, yang dapat mencatat kunjungan ke halaman ini sesuai kebijakan GitHub.

1. Who we are

Gemoy Grid Puzzle is made and operated by CodenameHRT, an independent developer in Indonesia. In this policy "we" means CodenameHRT. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: hasaychadan@gmail.com.

2. Summary

The game needs no account, email or phone number. It uses the internet for five things: the online leaderboard, progress backup for moving to another phone, Google AdMob ads, an app authenticity check (Google Play Integrity), and anonymous usage statistics. Your settings and best scores are also kept on your device.

3. What we process and why

a. Online leaderboard. When you finish a game, the game sends our server:

  • A random player ID (UUID) made on your device. It is not your real name, email or advertising ID.
  • The player name you choose (3 to 12 characters). It is unique (no two players share a name) and stays yours from then on. You can change it only in Settings by watching rewarded ads, and the number of ads you must watch goes up by one each time you change it. The server keeps the name, how many times it changed and when it last changed. Your name and score appear on the leaderboard that every player can see, so pick a name you are comfortable showing.
  • The score, number of moves, play time, game mode and time of the game.
  • A game record: the game's random seed and the sequence of moves with their times. It is used to check that the score was earned fairly (anti-cheat).
  • Your IP address at the time of the request, for security and abuse limiting.

Scores that could not be sent (for example while offline) are kept on the device and sent when the connection returns.

b. Progress backup (Settings > Backup / transfer). The server stores your player ID, stars, level, daily streak, days played and missions done, plus a hash of your random recovery code (not the code itself). The purpose is to restore your progress on another device. The recovery code is your secret: anyone who has it can restore your progress, so do not share it.

c. Ads. The game shows ads through Google AdMob. See section 5.

d. Technical data. Server logs (requests and errors) can contain the player ID and IP address. We use them for security and to fix problems.

e. Google Play. If you install from Google Play, Google gives us aggregated statistics and crash/ANR reports from users who turned on diagnostic data sharing. Google manages that.

f. App authenticity check (Google Play Integrity). When you send a score or choose or change your name, the game can ask Google Play on your device for a token. The token goes to our server, and we pass it to Google to check that the app is the official one from Google Play and that the device is genuine. Google processes signals about your device and the app for this check under Google's policies. We only log the result (passed or not) on the server, not the token. The purpose is security and preventing cheating. If the check is not available (for example without Google Play), the game can still be played.

g. Anonymous usage statistics. The game adds up on your device things like how often it was opened, play time, games per mode, ads shown and rewards taken, and sends only the sums to our server with the day number, the day the game was first installed, the game version and the name of a settings test group. This carries no player ID, name, advertising ID or device ID, and the server keeps only combined numbers per day, so they cannot be traced to you. The server sees your IP address at the time of the request (to limit abuse) but does not store it with the statistics. We use them to learn whether players come back and to tune the game.

h. Settings from the server. When it opens, the game fetches tuning numbers (for example the spacing between ads) from our server. The request carries no data about you.

4. Data kept only on your device

Local best scores, settings (sound, vibration, display, language), daily missions, the daily rewards already taken, and a play profile: your recent scores, when you last played and whether you often watch rewarded ads. The profile is used on the device to adjust difficulty and how often ads are offered. It is not sent to our server (except what section 3 lists).

5. Ads and consent (Google AdMob)

AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address, device and usage data, and ad interactions under Google's policies. Where required (for example the European Economic Area and the UK) the game shows a consent dialog (UMP) before ads load. You can change that choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google > Ads).

Rewarded ads are optional. We never ask for, push or reward ad clicks.

Android permissions the game requests: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, VIBRATE and AD_ID (advertising ID).

6. Legal bases

We process data based on: (1) your consent, for ads where required; (2) our legitimate interests, to run the leaderboard, keep it secure and prevent cheating; (3) providing the service you choose, for progress backup.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Google AdMob, for ads (section 5). Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Google Play Integrity API, to check the authenticity of the app and device (section 3f). Google's documentation: https://developer.android.com/google/play/integrity/other
  • The public, for your display name and score on the leaderboard.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services inside the game.

This policy page is served by GitHub Pages, which may log visits to this page under GitHub's policies.

8. International transfers

Google processes ad data in many countries. Our leaderboard server is run by the developer. By using the game you understand that data may be processed outside your country.

9. How long we keep data

  • Leaderboard entries, game records, your player name with its change count, and progress backup: while the leaderboard operates, or until you ask us to delete them. After deletion the name can be used by another player.
  • Daily server backups: kept 7 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Local data on your device: until you clear the app data or uninstall.

10. Your rights and how to ask for deletion

You have the right to ask for access, correction and deletion of your data, and to withdraw ad consent (see section 5), under applicable law, including Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) and, where it applies, the GDPR. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

To delete your name, scores and progress from our server, email hasaychadan@gmail.com with your display name and an approximate score or play date. To confirm the data is yours we may ask for the transfer code from Settings > Backup / transfer. We delete it within 60 days of receiving the request. Copies in the daily backups disappear within the next 7 days.

11. Children

This game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child gave us data, contact us so we can delete it.

12. Security

Data sent to our server uses HTTPS, recovery codes are stored only as a hash, and administrative access is limited by a token. No method of electronic storage or transmission is fully secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
